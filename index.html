About CampusForces

We're on a mission to make every college student in India a world-class programmer — one contest at a time.

△
Our mission
Democratize access to competitive programming for every college student — not just IITs and NITs.
◇
Our vision
A future where a student from any college in India can compete, grow, and get hired by their dream company.
■
Our values
Meritocracy, transparency, and relentless improvement. Your rank is earned — not bought.

Our story

CampusForces was born in 2022 inside a hostel room at DTU Delhi, when four friends noticed a painful gap: competitive programming was either inaccessible or intimidating for most students outside the top 10 institutes.

We built the first version in 6 weeks, ran our first contest with 80 participants, and haven't stopped since. Today, over 28,000 students across 300+ colleges compete on CampusForces every month.

Privacy policy

Last updated: May 2025. We keep this simple and honest.

What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and college name. During contests, we collect your code submissions and performance data to calculate your rank.

  • Account information (name, email, college)
  • Contest submissions and scores
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent) — anonymized
  • Cookie data only if you accept our cookie prompt

What we never do

  • We never sell your data to third parties
  • We never share your code with companies without your consent
  • We never store your payment information (we use Razorpay)

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in, and an analytics cookie (if accepted) to understand aggregate site usage. You can reject the analytics cookie at any time.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account, or opt out of any marketing at any time by emailing privacy@campusforces.in.

Terms of service

Last updated: May 2025. By using CampusForces, you agree to these terms.

Eligibility

CampusForces is open to students currently enrolled in any recognised college or university. You must be at least 16 years old to register.

Contest rules

  • You may not share your code with other participants during a live contest
  • You may not use AI tools to generate solutions during rated contests
  • Plagiarism detection runs on all submissions — repeated violations result in a permanent ban

Account

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. One person may not operate multiple accounts.

Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these terms, with or without prior notice.

Frequently asked questions

Is CampusForces free to use?+
Yes — 100% free for students. We monetise through our recruiter partnerships, not through students.
Which programming languages are supported?+
C, C++, Java, Python 3, JavaScript, Go, Rust, Kotlin, Swift, Ruby, PHP, and Haskell.
How is my rating calculated?+
We use an ELO-based system similar to Codeforces — your rating rises when you outperform expectations and falls when you underperform.
How do I report a problem error?+
Use the "Report issue" button inside the contest interface. Problem errors are reviewed within 30 minutes during live contests.
